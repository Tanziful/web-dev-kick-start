My Favorite Hobby : Reading Islamic books

My spiritual Journey through Islamic Literature

The beauty of Islamic knowledge

Reading Islamic books has been my most rewarding hobby for many years. It provides both spiritual nourishment and intellectual growth

My favourite Catagories

Among the many types of Islamic literature, I'm particularly drawn to:

  • Tafsir (Quranic exegesis)
  • Hadith collections
  • Seerah (Prophetic biography)
  • Fiqh (Islamic jurisprudence)

Recommended Readings

For beginners, I always suggest starting with "The Clear Quran" translation and "The Sealed Nectar" about the Prophet's life. These provide excellent foundations.

"Seeking knowledge is an obligation upon every Muslim."
- Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him)

Benefits I've Experienced

Through consistent reading, I've gained deeper understanding of my faith, developed patience and wisdom, and found practical guidance for daily life.

Creating a Reading Habit

Here are tips that helped me build this beneficial habit:

  1. Set aside time after Fajr prayer for reading
  2. Keep a notebook for reflections
  3. Join a study circle for accountability
  4. Start with short, manageable books that are too academic

Simple Egg Fried Rice

A quick and delicious meal ready in 15 minutes!

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups cooked rice (day-old works best)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, corn)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 spring onion (optional)

instructions:

  1. Heat oil in a pan or wok on medium heat
  2. Add vegetables and stir-fry for 2 minutes
  3. Push vegetables to one side, crack eggs into the pan
  4. Scramble the eggs until cooked, then mix with vegetables
  5. Add rice, soy sauce, salt and pepper
  6. Stir-fry everything together for 3-4 minutes
  7. Garnish with chopped spring onion (if using)

Cooking Tips

Day-old rice
Works best as it's drier and won't get mushy
High heat
Keep the heat medium-high for best results
Variations
Add chicken, shrimp or tofu for extra protein
